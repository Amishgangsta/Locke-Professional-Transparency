# Production agreement-library coverage

The reviewed product supports governed legal-content libraries with release/readiness controls and categorized template search.

| Collection | Representative matters |
|---|---|
| Confidentiality and information protection | NDAs, information handling, permitted-use restrictions |
| Creative, marketing, and IP | IP rights, campaign services, licensing and delivery |
| Construction and trades | Subcontractor relationships, projects, changes and acceptance |
| Employment and people | Workforce and engagement terms |
| Money and risk allocation | Financial and liability terms |
| Real estate and property | Leasing and property arrangements |
| Services and consulting | Services agreements, consulting, scopes of work |
| Sales, purchasing, and supply | Purchase and supply terms |
| Software and technology | Software, technology and related services |

## Why this matters

A document starts from a curated template or governed content family, subject to release and readiness decisions. The firm can inspect clause families and proceed through structured questions; the platform does not assume every registered internal content record is production-ready.
